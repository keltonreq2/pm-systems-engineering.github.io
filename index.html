01 / Profile

A practical foundation for engineering.

Welcome to my portfolio. My name is Patrice Masson. I am an engineer in training at ENSEEIHT in the 3EA department and an electrotechnical professional at EDF, where the source portfolio describes more than 15 years of experience.

My work has brought together field operations, electrical systems, protection and control-command, technical training, and the modernization of industrial installations. I am now strengthening that experience through engineering studies.

This portfolio preserves the substance of my previous English-language portfolio while reorganising it around my engineering path and technical projects.
